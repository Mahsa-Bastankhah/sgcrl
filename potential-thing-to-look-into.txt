There is also a termination difference:

Baseline separately represents environment termination and truncation.
Shaped PPO places termination and truncation into one done mask.
Shaped PPO therefore does not distinguish a true terminal state from a time-limit truncation in GAE.
Baseline masks truncation transitions rather than handling them exactly like ordinary terminal transitions.
